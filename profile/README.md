## OU CARE AI

AI research, hands-on workshops, and research computing at Oakland University.
[Website](https://ou-care-ai.github.io/)

### Advancing AI Research with NAIRR

A workshop series on cybersecurity, edge AI, and autonomous driving by Oakland University and Worcester
Polytechnic Institute, supported by the NSF NAIRR Pilot Expansion (awards #2528533 and #2528534). Twelve
hands-on workshops over two years, all runnable in a browser on Jetstream2.

[Workshop series site](https://ou-care-ai.github.io/nairr-eager/) ·
[Workshop list](https://ou-care-ai.github.io/nairr-eager/#workshops) ·
[Project team](https://ou-care-ai.github.io/nairr-eager/#team)

### Research computing

The project's Jetstream2 allocation also supports a small number of Oakland University and WPI research
groups working with the project, by invitation. Reach out to the project team to ask about access.
